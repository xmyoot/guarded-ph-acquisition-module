# Sources and third-party material

Native circuit design, layout, documentation and firmware were developed for this prototype with AI assistance. No original Altium design was supplied or reproduced. Client screenshots are local input evidence and are not included in the public repository.

Project-local standard footprint geometry was copied/adapted from KiCad's libraries, distributed under CC-BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad libraries exception. See https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/ and https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-footprints . The BNC footprint was adapted to Amphenol drawing 031-5431-1010 Rev E. The custom detailed BNC visualization is drawing-derived, approximate and not supplied by Amphenol. Standard rendered 3D models come from the installed KiCad libraries.

Manufacturer datasheet PDFs are not relicensed by this project and are omitted from public source control. Source URLs and reviewed-document hashes are retained in datasheets/sources.json and docs/architecture/06_sources.md. kicad-happy and KiCadRoutingTools are external tools with their own licenses, not bundled code.
